## Scene-map / blockout / latent-space research

The feasibility result is an editable **Scene Intent Graph**: geometric proxies
or optional point samples, stable object identities, semantics, typed relations,
variants/time, and explicit constraints. A literal fifth axis for mood is not a
useful production representation. Mood remains an authored target with visual
evidence and human review; embeddings can help retrieval.

The proposed MVP is one room or compact point of interest, with reversible
graybox realization and measured designer utility. Major blockers are unloaded
World Partition coverage, stable identity/reconciliation, and PLUMB checks that
currently approximate clearance by point centers and grounding by z=0.

Research and stop conditions are recorded in
[`13-SCENE-INTENT-GRAPH-FEASIBILITY.md`](../design/2026-08-23-extension-redesign/13-SCENE-INTENT-GRAPH-FEASIBILITY.md).
The graph/point-cloud editor and realization pipeline are not implemented.
