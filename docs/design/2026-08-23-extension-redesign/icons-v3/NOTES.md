The 16 assets were generated individually with the built-in AI image generator from prompts derived from the Hayba logo’s broad, rounded, soft-symmetrical silhouette and cream/ochre band logic, then downscaled to 512 px and palette-normalized to exact flat fills (`#DED4C3` and `#C47A28`) while retaining the generated geometry and transparent alpha. `rule-violated` fought the flat-shape contract by introducing dark smudging, so it was regenerated with a stricter pristine-fill prompt and its interior alpha was flattened; no icon was replaced with hand-authored SVG geometry.
