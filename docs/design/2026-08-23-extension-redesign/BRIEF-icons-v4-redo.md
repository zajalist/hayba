# Icon redo — v4 (user cull)

Same pipeline (image_gen → System.Drawing palette-normalize to flat `#DED4C3`
/ `#C47A28`, transparent, 512px). Same style contract: filled silhouette, no
outlines/strokes/gradients/shadows, chunky rounded folk-geometric in the
Hayba logo's language, strictly two-tone, bold features that survive 28px.
Lead with the METAPHOR. **TWO candidates per icon** (`<name>-a.png`,
`<name>-b.png`) varying the metaphor treatment.

REJECTED directions are listed per icon — never redraw them.

## Nouns / semantics

1. **world** (rejected: soft hill lump) — (a) a horizon arc with a rising
   sun disc, sun ochre; (b) a rounded standing-stone ring seen from above,
   one stone ochre. Must read as "a place/terrain", not a pebble.
2. **library** (rejected: pebble stack) — (a) three chunky book spines in a
   row, middle one ochre; (b) an archive chest with two drawers, one drawer
   ochre.
3. **rules** (rejected: tablet, plumb bob, balance, level) — (a) a rounded
   stone ARCH/gateway with an ochre keystone (what holds everything up);
   (b) a banner/standard on a pole with an ochre emblem (the law you march
   under).
4. **chat** (rejected: bubble with dot-tail that reads as a notification) —
   (a) two overlapping rounded speech bubbles, back one ochre, NO tail dots;
   (b) one bold bubble with a proper wedge tail (part of the silhouette, not
   a separate dot).
5. **profile** (rejected: vase, tag) — (a) an ID-card shape: rounded plate
   with a circle + two bars carved in, circle ochre; (b) an object standing
   on a measured plinth with tick marks, plinth ochre.
6. **undo** (rejected: curled arrow) — (a) one bold clean U-turn arrow,
   uniform thickness, ochre head; (b) a footprint pair walking back, one
   ochre. Prefer (a) unless (b) lands perfectly.

## Actions

7. **reject** (rejected: X with random ochre tips) — one bold uniform X,
   all cream; candidate (b): X inside a rounded square, square cream, X
   ochre.
8. **save** (rejected: lidded pot) — (a) a strongbox/chest with an ochre
   latch; (b) a grain sack tied at the top, tie ochre (stored = kept).
9. **refresh** (rejected: single circle arrow) — (a) two chunky arrows
   chasing in a circle, one ochre; (b) a water-wheel form with an ochre
   paddle.
10. **connect** (rejected: chain links) — (a) two rounded plug halves
    docking, joint ochre; (b) a rope splice: two thick rope ends merging,
    wrap ochre.
11. **build** (rejected: floating hammer) — (a) hammer face-down striking a
    block, contact wedge ochre; (b) three stacked masonry blocks with the
    top one being placed, that block ochre.

## Domains

12. **terrain** (rejected: wavy strata biscuit) — (a) a stepped mesa /
    plateau silhouette, one stratum ochre; (b) a cut-away ground block:
    flat top line, two clear strata below, middle stratum ochre.
13. **material** (rejected: paint dab on tile) — (a) a 2x2 checker tile,
    two cream two ochre squares, rounded; (b) a draped cloth swatch over a
    bar, cloth ochre.
14. **python** (rejected: spiral with dark line) — (a) a coiled snake form,
    STRICTLY two flat tones, head wedge ochre, NO eyes, NO dark lines;
    (b) an S-curve thick serpent band, head ochre.
15. **camera-viewport** (rejected: frame with corner nubs) — (a) four bold
    corner brackets only, one bracket ochre; (b) a rounded frame with an
    ochre focus wedge in one corner.

## State marks (all rejected: triangle / clock-wedge / plain dot — they read
as notification badges). These are tiny inline glyphs (14-16px next to text,
NEVER composited on icons). Design them as a matched family — same weight,
same size feel:

16. **state-attention** — a small folk pennant flag on a stub pole, flag
    ochre; (b) a bold rounded exclamation stroke+dot as ONE fused shape.
17. **state-pending** — a small hourglass, one bulb ochre; (b) a half-filled
    rising sun at horizon (waiting for it).
18. **state-unsaved** — the logo's own SPARK mark (the small 4-point star
    above the hut in HaybaLogo.svg) in ochre — brand-native "not yet
    settled"; (b) an open loop / unfinished circle with a gap.
